This page is intentionally repetitive. It provides enough headings and vertical space to test the collapsed skeleton, the expanded table of contents, active section tracking, navigation, wrapping, and behavior near the end of an article.

## Start

The first section has a very short label. Hover over the skeleton cluster, move the pointer in and out several times, and confirm that every label appears in a single smooth expansion without pushing the article horizontally.

Scroll slowly until the next heading reaches the reading position. The active marker should move from this entry to the next one without changing the width or height of any skeleton bar.

This section represents the common case: a heading that fits comfortably on one line. Click its TOC entry from another part of the page and confirm that the hash changes and the heading lands at the expected offset.

The text here adds breathing room between anchors. It also makes it easier to see whether active tracking updates too early, too late, or differently when scrolling upward.
